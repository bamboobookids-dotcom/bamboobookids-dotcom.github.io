Privacy Policy

Kids Shorts Studio · Last updated 12 October 2026

Who we are

Kids Shorts Studio ("we") makes original animated videos for children and publishes them on our own channels: Kitty Bun Bun, Poppy Panda & Friends and Tiny Town Heroes. Kids Shorts Studio is also the name of the private software tool we use to upload and schedule those videos.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from viewers, and we never knowingly collect personal information from children. Our videos are marked as made for kids where the platform offers this setting, and we do not use our videos to gather data about the people who watch them.

How the Kids Shorts Studio app uses platform data

The app connects only to accounts that we own and manage. With our permission, it uses:

  • YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): to upload our videos to our own YouTube channels, set their title, description, made-for-kids and AI-content settings, schedule them, and read our own channel name.
  • Meta Graph API (Facebook Pages and Instagram): to publish our videos as Reels on our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.
  • TikTok Content Posting API: to publish our videos to our own TikTok accounts, labelled as AI-generated.

The app does not read, store or share viewers' data, comments, messages or analytics. Access tokens are stored only on our own computer and are never sent to anyone other than the platform that issued them.

Use of Google user data

Kids Shorts Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use YouTube API Services; by using our channels you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information. We do not use any data for advertising profiles.

Removing access

Access we granted to our own app can be removed at any time from Google account permissions, Facebook business integrations settings or TikTok app permissions. If you believe we hold any information about you or your child, contact us and we will delete it.
